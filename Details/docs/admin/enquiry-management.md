# Inquiry Management System Operations

## 1. Staff Assignment & Ownership
- New inquiries are automatically assigned based on availability or claimed by senior expedition planners.
- System logs time of first response to enforce the **12-Hour Luxury Service Level Agreement (SLA)**.

## 2. Interaction Logging & Notes
- Staff members can log call summaries, WhatsApp conversation highlights, custom pricing quotes, and special dietary/accessibility requirements.
- Automated reminder alerts trigger when an inquiry reaches its scheduled follow-up date without an update.
