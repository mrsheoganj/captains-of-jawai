# Final Project Acceptance Criteria

The website build will be considered complete and production-ready when:
- [ ] Brand logo (`logo.PNG`) renders crisply in desktop header, mobile header, and footer without distortion.
- [ ] No direct online checkout or cart functionality exists; all conversion actions route to consultative inquiry.
- [ ] Multi-step inquiry form submits cleanly via AJAX, storing the lead in MariaDB and dispatching email notifications.
- [ ] Admin panel allows logging in, managing lead statuses in the CRM, adding staff notes, and editing content.
- [ ] Mobile sticky bottom dock functions smoothly on all iOS and Android devices.
- [ ] Zero fabricated reviews, fake awards, or guaranteed sighting claims appear anywhere on the site.
- [ ] All images used are authentic, legally licensed or CC-cleared, with rights logged in the asset ledger.
- [ ] Google Lighthouse mobile score achieves >= 95 across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.
