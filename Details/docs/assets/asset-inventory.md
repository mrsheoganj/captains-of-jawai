# Brand Asset Inventory & Specifications

## 1. Master Brand Logo (`logo.PNG`)
- **Master Asset File:** Located at `assets/logo.PNG` and `docs/assets/logo.PNG`.
- **Visual Elements Present in Master Logo:**
  - *Anchor Symbol:* Nautical/expedition leadership emblem ("The Captains"), central axis with stylized geometric diamond cutouts.
  - *Apex Predator (Indian Leopard):* Highly detailed profile of a leopard on the right shoulder with characteristic rosette patterns and watchful amber eye.
  - *Mugger Crocodile:* Perched across the horizontal crossbeam of the anchor with jaws agape, representing the Jawai Bandh reptile sanctuary.
  - *Granite Hills & Sunrise:* Monolithic bornhardt formation in the background encircled by a golden sun crest at the top apex.
  - *Expedition 4x4 Jeep:* Customized green safari vehicle climbing the boulder slope on the left flank.
  - *Custom Typography:* Distinctive stylized dual-line serif lettering: "CAPTAINS" (arched top), "OF" (center), and "JAWAI" (bold geometric base with central chevron glyph).
- **Required File Formats for Implementation:**
  - `logo-master.png`: High-resolution original graphic.
  - `logo-header.png`: Optimized transparent PNG sized for desktop header (height: 56px–64px).
  - `logo-mobile.png`: Compact transparent version for mobile header (height: 40px–44px).
  - `favicon.ico` / `favicon.svg` / `apple-touch-icon.png`: Derived circular crest for browser tab identity.
  - `og-image.jpg`: 1200x630px social share banner pairing the logo mark with a dramatic granite background.

## 2. Iconography Strategy
- **Format:** Pure inline SVG or crisp SVG sprite system (zero external heavy icon font libraries like FontAwesome).
- **Style:** 1.5px hairline stroke, rounded joints, matching the muted amber (`#C8963E`) and ivory (`#F5F3EC`) color tokens.
- **Core Icon Set Required:**
  - 4x4 Safari Jeep
  - Binoculars / Optical Gear
  - Camera / Telephoto Lens
  - Granite Kopje / Mountain Peak
  - Leopard Pawprint / Track
  - Waterbird / Flamingo
  - Compass Rose / Cardinal Indicator
  - Calendar / Date Picker
  - Shield / Conservation Seal
  - WhatsApp & Phone Communication Marks
