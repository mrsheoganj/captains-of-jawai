# Customer Journey Mapping

```
[ STAGE 1: INSPIRATION & DISCOVERY ]
Channels: Instagram Reel, Travel Article, Word of Mouth, Google Search ("best leopard safari in rajasthan")
Mindset: "I want to see leopards in the wild, but I don't want a crowded national park zoo experience."
Touchpoint: Lands on Captains of Jawai Homepage (Cinematic hero video/stills, editorial aesthetic).

[ STAGE 2: IMMERSION & EVALUATION ]
Exploration: Reviews "The Leopards of the Rocks", "The Captains (Guides)", "Safari Experiences", and "Gallery".
Reassurance: Discovers strict ethical wildlife standards, private 4x4 exclusivity, and authentic Rabari coexistence story.
Overcoming Objections: Reads FAQ regarding sighting probabilities, weather by season, and travel access from Udaipur/Jodhpur.

[ STAGE 3: INQUIRY & CONSULTATION ]
Action: Clicks "Plan Your Journey" or "WhatsApp Expedition Team".
Form Interaction: Completes intuitive multi-step form (dates, guests, safari interests, lodging tier).
Immediate Feedback: Automated personalized email acknowledgment stating expectations ("Your Captain will respond within 12 hours").

[ STAGE 4: BESPOKE PROPOSAL & CONVERSION ]
Contact: Senior expedition manager reaches out via WhatsApp / phone consultation.
Deliverable: Customized PDF itinerary featuring recommended safari schedule, private sundowner spots, and transfer logistics.
Confirmation: Offline payment / advance deposit secured via direct bank transfer or authorized merchant link.

[ STAGE 5: ON-GROUND EXPEDITION & POST-TRIP ADVOCACY ]
Arrival: Seamless private transfer from airport to lodge.
Experience: Flawless private safaris with dedicated Captain, high optical standards, memorable encounters.
Advocacy: High-resolution photo sharing, verified Google review submission, word-of-mouth referral to high-net-worth network.
```
