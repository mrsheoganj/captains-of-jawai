# Brand Tone of Voice & Editorial Guidelines

## 1. Voice Attributes
- **Authoritative yet Humble:** Speaks with the quiet confidence of seasoned naturalists who respect the unpredictability of nature. Never boastful; never guarantees sightings.
- **Cinematic & Evocative:** Uses rich sensory language (the warmth of sun-baked granite, the resonant bark of a langur alarm call, the crimson clash of a Rabari turban against grey stone).
- **Refined & Understated:** Luxury is expressed through meticulous attention to detail and curated experiences, not loud adjectives.
- **Respectful & Ethical:** Treats wildlife as wild subjects deserving space, not spectacle props. Speaks of the Rabari with cultural reverence.

## 2. Vocabulary & Style Matrix
| Recommended Language | Words & Phrases to STRICTLY AVOID |
| :--- | :--- |
| Granite kopjes, cavernous boulders, resident leopards | "Cheap safari", "Lowest price guarantee", "Best deal" |
| Tracking, observation, naturalist, expedition | "Jeep rental", "Leopard spotting service", "Safari ride" |
| Bespoke itinerary, curated journey, private expedition | "Package deal", "Standard tour", "Budget safari" |
| Living coexistence, pastoral heritage, ancient pact | "Tribal show", "Village entertainment", "Tourist trap" |
| High observation probability, ethical viewing distance | "100% Guaranteed leopard sighting", "Guaranteed kill" |
| Basking, rim-lit granite, twilight drives | "Rush safari", "Speed tour", "Quick look" |
