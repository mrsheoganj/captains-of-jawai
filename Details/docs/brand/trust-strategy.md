# Trust Architecture & Credibility Framework

## 1. Trust Signals for High-End Travel
To establish immediate credibility without resorting to fabricated reviews:
1. **Named Field Leadership:** Profiles of head naturalists and trackers showcasing years of tracking experience, optical gear knowledge, and local geography familiarity.
2. **Transparent Operational Standards:** Clear documentation of vehicle specifications (custom 4x4 suspension, tiered ergonomic seating, charging ports, beanbag mounts).
3. **Rigorous Conservation Ethics:** Published code of conduct for animal distance, vehicle counts at sightings, and community benefit sharing.
4. **Transparent Inquiry Process:** Setting realistic expectations (e.g., "Inquiry received → 12-hour response by a senior expedition planner → Custom itinerary proposal → Secure confirmation").
5. **Real Photography Guarantee:** Explicit commitment to showing only authentic, unmanipulated photographs captured in Jawai and Bera.
