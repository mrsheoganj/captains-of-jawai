# Accommodation & Hospitality Competitor Matrix

While Captains of Jawai focuses primarily on safari expeditions, wilderness discovery, and bespoke itineraries, understanding accommodation partners vs. competitors is vital:

| Property Tier | Examples | Relationship to Captains of Jawai | Collaboration / Strategic Approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Ultra-Luxury Wilderness Camps** | SUJÁN Jawai | Self-contained competitor (rarely outsources safaris). | Respectful peer benchmark; differentiate by offering independent bespoke expeditions. |
| **Tier 2: Luxury Boutique Resorts** | Cheetahgarh Resort, Brij Pola, Amritara Jawai Sagar | Potential Lodging Partner / Competitor. | Partner for luxury stay packages where Captains of Jawai provides dedicated private naturalist expeditions. |
| **Tier 3: Heritage Havelis & Forts** | Castle Bera, Rawla Bisalpur | Collaborative Partner. | Ideal pairing for guests seeking historic Rajput hospitality combined with top-tier modern wildlife tracking. |
| **Tier 4: Safari Camps & Eco-Lodges** | Jawai Nature Stay, Leopard Lair | Lodging Partner. | High-quality accommodation base for value-luxury travellers. |
