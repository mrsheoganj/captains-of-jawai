# Social Media & Instagram Competitor Analysis

## 1. Current State of Social Media in Jawai
- **The "Reels" Boom:** Jawai has experienced massive visibility on Instagram and YouTube over the past 36 months, driven by viral drone shots of 4x4 jeeps climbing 45-degree granite boulders and close-up videos of leopards lounging on village temple steps.
- **Dominant Content Types:**
  - *Action Safari Shorts:* 4x4 crawling on granite rocks with dramatic audio tracks.
  - *Leopard Sighting Highlights:* Telephoto footage of cubs playing outside granite caves.
  - *Rabari Portraits:* Striking portraits of red-turbaned elders with camels against sunset skies.
  - *Luxury Sundowners:* Champagne glasses on boulder peaks overlooking Jawai Bandh.

## 2. Competitor Social Presence
- **Top Operator Accounts:** ~10k to 50k followers, but largely informal, lacking consistent brand aesthetic, font hierarchy, or cohesive colour palette.
- **SUJÁN Life:** ~180k followers; masterclass in slow, restrained, black-and-white and muted-earth photography; high engagement with international conservationists.

## 3. Social Integration Strategy for Captains of Jawai
- The website must integrate an **editorial-grade Instagram gallery / stories ticker** that pulls verified authentic captures.
- Maintain a clear brand aesthetic: moody earth tones, granite grey, amber sunsets, and crisp typography rather than noisy, over-saturated mobile videos.
