# Content Strategy & Editorial Framework

## 1. Content Pillars
1. **The Wildlife & The Science:** Deep, fascinating, biologically accurate stories regarding leopard behavior, territorial dynamics, and prey ecology in granite environments.
2. **The Human-Leopard Coexistence:** Nuanced anthropological explorations of the Rabari community, temple folklore, and conservation realities.
3. **The Practical Safari Field Guides:** Comprehensive planning guides covering gear, weather, photography optics, clothing, and travel logistics from Udaipur, Jodhpur, and Jaipur.
4. **Conservation & Ethical Tourism:** Educational content establishing why ethical distance, vehicle limits, and silence at sightings protect wildlife and preserve the magic of Jawai.
