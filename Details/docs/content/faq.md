# Comprehensive Master FAQ Repository

## Category 1: Safari Mechanics & Wildlife Sightings
- **Q: Are leopard sightings guaranteed in Jawai?**  
  *A:* In nature, ethical wildlife sightings can never be 100% guaranteed, and any operator promising a guaranteed sighting is misleading you. However, Jawai boasts one of the highest leopard sighting probabilities anywhere in the world. Because the leopards reside on open granite rocks rather than in impenetrable jungle foliage, daytime and twilight observation rates during a typical 2 to 3-day stay exceed 90%.
- **Q: How does a safari in Jawai differ from Ranthambhore or Jim Corbett?**  
  *A:* Unlike enclosed national parks with rigid government gate quotas, predetermined route zones, and mandatory safari canters, Jawai is an open conservation landscape. Safaris are conducted in private, customized 4x4 open-top vehicles with complete freedom to traverse private granite kopjes, sandy riverbeds, and reservoir shores led by expert local trackers.

## Category 2: Planning & Logistics
- **Q: When is the best time of year to visit Jawai?**  
  *A:* The prime season spans from **October through March**, when morning and evening temperatures are crisp (12°C–26°C), leopards bask openly on the rocks for warmth, and tens of thousands of migratory birds populate Jawai Dam. The summer months (April–June) are hotter but offer phenomenal, concentrated wildlife sightings around remaining waterholes.
- **Q: How do we reach Jawai?**  
  *A:* The nearest major airports are Udaipur (UDR, ~2.5 hours scenic drive) and Jodhpur (JDH, ~2.5 hours drive). The nearest major railway junction is Falna (FA, ~35 minutes), which receives daily Rajdhani and express trains from Delhi, Mumbai, and Jaipur. Captains of Jawai arranges private, luxury air-conditioned road transfers from all airports and stations.

## Category 3: Ethics & Safety
- **Q: Is it safe to be near wild leopards in an open 4x4 vehicle?**  
  *A:* Yes. Jawai's leopards have coexisted with humans and open vehicles for generations without any record of unprovoked attacks on safari guests. Our Captains adhere to strict distance protocols, never block animal escape routes, and turn off engines to maintain calm, non-intrusive viewing.
