# Legal Image Rights, Licensing & Sourcing Framework

## 1. The Legal Reality of Pinterest (Critical Client Guidance)
- **Can Pinterest images be used directly on the website?**  
  **NO.** Pinterest is a content aggregator and bookmarking social network. Virtually all images on Pinterest are copyrighted by original photographers, safari lodges, or magazines. Downloading and publishing images from Pinterest onto a commercial company website violates international copyright law, exposes the business to DMCA takedown notices, statutory damages, and catastrophic brand embarrassment.
- **What is Pinterest good for?**  
  Pinterest should be used strictly as an **internal visual moodboard** and aesthetic reference tool to guide lighting, composition, wardrobe, and vehicle styling for the brand's own original photo shoots.

## 2. Permitted & Verified Copyright-Free / Commercial-Friendly Sources
For launch and MVP phases, Captains of Jawai can legally use:
1. **Direct Client-Owned Original Photography (Tier 1 - Preferred):** High-resolution photos shot in Jawai by the brand's founders, field team, or hired local photographers. Complete rights owned.
2. **Wikimedia Commons (Creative Commons Attribution CC-BY & CC-BY-SA):** Authentic, verified historical and ecological photographs of Jawai Dam, Rajasthan wildlife, and Rabari culture. Requires strict machine-readable attribution.
3. **Flickr Creative Commons (Commercial Permitted):** Wildlife photographers who release Rajasthan wildlife under CC BY 2.0.
4. **Public Domain / CC0 Platforms (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay):** General contextual imagery (e.g., wild leopards in natural habitat, acacia scrub, vintage 4x4 jeep mechanics, campfire sundowners) with zero attribution requirement and safe commercial licenses.

## 3. Image Rights Documentation Standard
Every single production image must be registered in `docs/assets/image-rights.md` with source URL, photographer name, license type, commercial validity, and required attribution text.
