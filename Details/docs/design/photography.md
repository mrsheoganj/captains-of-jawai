# Photography Strategy, Curation & Standards

## 1. Visual Integrity & Zero AI Wildlife Policy
- **Absolute Rule:** No AI-synthesized wildlife images. Discerning wildlife travellers immediately detect anatomically flawed whiskers, distorted claws, and synthetic fur rendering, which instantly destroys brand trust.
- **Tone:** Real, atmospheric, documentary, and cinematographic.
- **Lighting Preference:** Golden-hour dawn (first 90 minutes of sunlight), twilight rim-lighting on granite boulders, and atmospheric dust suspended in safari vehicle headlights.

## 2. Core Image Categories Required
1. **The Leopards of the Rocks:** Real photographs of Indian leopards (*Panthera pardus fusca*) resting on smooth granite kopjes, peering from cave crevices, or patrolling stony ridges.
2. **The Ancient Landscape:** Sweeping panoramic vistas of Jawai's weathered bornhardts, thorny desert scrub, and dramatic sunrise/sunset skies.
3. **The Wetland & Dam Ecosystem:** Jawai Bandh waters, resident mugger crocodiles sunning on sandy shoals, and flocks of pink Greater Flamingos.
4. **The Rabari Guardians:** Dignified, respectful portraits of Rabari herdsmen in scarlet turbans, white tunics, and silver jewelry with their camel herds.
5. **The Expedition Fleet:** Open-top, custom 4x4 safari vehicles traversing granite inclines, equipped with naturalists holding telephoto lenses or binoculars.
6. **Curated Luxury Moments:** Private sundowner setups on a secluded rock summit, fresh artisanal tea served from brass kettles, and campfire lanterns under the desert stars.
