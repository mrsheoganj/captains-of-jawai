## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose
Captains of Jawai is conceived as an ultra-premium, inquiry-led wildlife safari and bespoke wilderness expedition brand operating in the granite hill country of Jawai and Bera, Pali District, Rajasthan. 

Unlike transactional booking engines that reduce one of the planet's most fascinating wildlife landscapes into high-turnover "jeep rentals", Captains of Jawai is positioned at the intersection of **conservation storytelling, human-wildlife coexistence, luxury slow travel, and ethical expedition leadership**.

### Core Tenet
The wilderness of Jawai is not an enclosed national park; it is a community-owned mosaic of billion-year-old pre-Cambrian granite formations (*kopjes*), private farmland, pastoral scrub, and the vast reservoir of Jawai Bandh. In this landscape, free-ranging Indian leopards (*Panthera pardus fusca*) thrive in peaceful coexistence with the semi-nomadic Rabari pastoralists. 

Captains of Jawai provides discerning Indian and international travellers with an intimately guided, low-impact, highly personalized wilderness journey led by expert local trackers and naturalists.

## 2. Business Model Architecture: Inquiry-Led Discovery
The business model strictly rejects instant online booking ("click, pay, book") for the MVP:
1. **No Algorithmic Slot Booking:** Wildlife sightings, vehicle permissions, private zone access, and luxury lodge logistics in Jawai cannot be managed reliably via automated, unmediated checkout.
2. **Consultative Curation:** Every trip involves tailoring the balance between morning/evening leopard tracking, Jawai Bandh migratory wetland drives, Rabari village cultural walks, and bespoke bush dining.
3. **High-Value Lead Generation:** The website's primary conversion metric is the submission of a qualified **"Plan Your Journey"** or **"Speak With an Expedition Captain"** inquiry, capturing dates, party composition, wildlife priorities, accommodation style, and special requirements.
4. **Offline Fulfillment:** Confirmation of accommodation, dedicated 4x4 open safari jeeps, transfers (from Udaipur/Jodhpur/Ahmedabad), and itineraries occurs via high-touch concierge follow-up (WhatsApp Business, phone consultations, and customized PDF proposals).

## 3. Grounding Verification & Data Integrity
To preserve complete integrity and avoid legal, commercial, or ethical liability:
- **No Fabricated Pricing:** Public rates vary by season, vehicle exclusivity, and lodging level. Baseline jeep permits and safari costs are noted from published market ranges, but exact package rates are flagged as **`CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED`**.
- **No Guaranteed Sighting Claims:** In accordance with IUCN and ethical safari standards, sightings are never guaranteed. The website communicates exceptional sighting probabilities based on Jawai's unique granite topography and resident leopard tracking history.
- **No AI Wildlife Imagery:** All production visual assets must be authentic, copyright-cleared photographs depicting Jawai's actual granite boulders, resident leopards, Jawai Bandh waters, Rabari herdsmen, and customized 4x4 expedition vehicles.
- **Client Verification Requirements:** Legal entity registration, official registered address, GSTIN, emergency contacts, insurance policies, and exact field team credentials are comprehensively indexed in `docs/CLIENT_INPUT_REQUIRED.md`.
