# Tourism Ecosystem & Visitor Infrastructure

## 1. Access Infrastructure & Gateway Hubs
- **Airports:**
  - **Udaipur (Maharana Pratap Airport - UDR):** ~135 km / 2.5 to 3 hours scenic road drive via NH 27. Most popular for luxury leisure travellers.
  - **Jodhpur (Civil Airport - JDH):** ~150 km / 2.5 to 3 hours road drive via RJ SH 62 / NH 62. Excellent connectivity for heritage Rajasthan circuits.
  - **Ahmedabad (SVP International Airport - AMD):** ~280 km / 4.5 to 5 hours via express highway. Major international gateway.
- **Railways:**
  - **Falna (FA):** Major railway station (28 km / 35 minutes from Bera/Jawai) with premium superfast and Rajdhani express trains connecting Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, and Ahmedabad.
  - **Jawai Bandh (JWB):** Local station handling select intercity passenger trains.

## 2. Accommodation Landscape
The lodging ecosystem in Jawai spans distinct tiers:
1. **Ultra-Luxury Wilderness Camps:** e.g., SUJÁN Jawai (~₹1,20,000 to ₹1,80,000+ per night inclusive of safaris). Focus on privacy, bespoke naturalists, and Relais & Châteaux standards.
2. **Luxury Boutique Lodges & Resorts:** e.g., WelcomHeritage Cheetahgarh, Brij Pola Jawai, Jawai Sagar, Amritara (~₹25,000 to ₹55,000 per night). Luxury stone villas, private plunge pools, fine dining.
3. **Heritage Havelis & Castle Stays:** e.g., Castle Bera, Rawla Bisalpur (~₹12,000 to ₹25,000 per night). Historic aristocratic ambiance, royal family hosting.
4. **Mid-Range & Safari Camps:** e.g., Varawal Leopard Camp, Jawai Nature Stay, Jawai Safari Camp (~₹6,000 to ₹15,000 per night). Authentic safari-focused tents and cottages.
5. **Budget Homestays & Guest Houses:** (~₹2,500 to ₹5,000 per night). Local family run.

## 3. Safari Regulation & Mechanics
- **Vehicles:** Custom 4x4 open-top safari vehicles (primarily customized Mahindra Thar, Maruti Gypsy, or customized safari campers) equipped with tiered seating, high ground clearance, roll cages, camera beanbags, and specialized suspension for rock crawling.
- **Timings:**
  - **Morning Safari:** 05:30/06:00 to 09:00/09:30 (Sunrise tracking on rocks).
  - **Evening Safari:** 16:00/16:30 to 19:30/20:00 (Sunset tracking & twilight spotlighting).
  - **Wetland / Dam Drive:** 08:30 to 12:00 (Crocodiles, flamingos, migratory birds).
