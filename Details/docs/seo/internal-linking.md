          └─────────── [ 50 JOURNAL POSTS ] ────┘
              (Every article passes link equity

## 1. Internal Linking Rules
- **No Orphan Pages:** Every page must have at least 2 inbound links from contextual parent or peer pages.
- **Descriptive Anchor Text:** Never use generic "click here" or "learn more". Always use rich descriptive anchors (e.g., *explore our private 4x4 leopard safari*, *learn about Jawai Bandh crocodile watching*).
