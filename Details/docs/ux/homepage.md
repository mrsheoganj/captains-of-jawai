# Comprehensive Homepage UX & Editorial Specification

## Section 1: Cinematic Hero Showcase
- **Objective:** Immediate emotional immersion in Jawai's granite wilderness.
- **Visual:** High-resolution full-bleed cinematic image or ambient video showing a leopard atop a massive golden granite boulder at sunrise.
- **Copy:**
  - *Kicker:* `25°06'N · 73°10'E — PALI DISTRICT, RAJASTHAN`
  - *H1:* The Kingdom of Granite and Gold.
  - *Subline:* Private, naturalist-led wildlife expeditions across the ancient leopard hills of Jawai.
- **CTAs:**
  - *Primary Button:* "Plan Your Journey" (Anchors or opens consultation intake).
  - *Secondary Ghost Button:* "Explore Safaris" (Smooth scrolls to Section 4).

## Section 2: Real-Time Field Ticker / Marquee
- **Objective:** Convey immediate authenticity, dynamic field presence, and conservation focus.
- **Content String:** `Bespoke 4x4 Expeditions · 60+ Resident Leopards · Over 300 Bird Species · Zero Sighting Guarantees / 100% Ethical Tracking · Sacred Rabari Coexistence · Jawai Bandh Wetland Reserve`.

## Section 3: The Narrative Hook — "Why Jawai is Unlike Any Place on Earth"
- **Objective:** Educate the visitor on why Jawai is superior to crowded national parks.
- **Layout:** Editorial 2-column split.
- **Left Column:** Headline: "Where apex predators walk among sacred temples and nomadic herdsmen."
- **Right Column:** Narrative prose detailing the billion-year-old granite kopjes, the absence of rigid government gate zones, the intimate 4x4 experience, and the sacred pact between leopards and the Rabari.

## Section 4: Signature Safari Expeditions (Interactive Cards)
- **Objective:** Present core safari formats with clear differentiation.
- **Layout:** 3-column responsive card grid.
  1. *The Classic Leopard Tracking Safari* (Dawn & Dusk granite cave exploration).
  2. *The Jawai Bandh Wetland & Raptor Drive* (Crocodiles, flamingos, cranes).
  3. *The Master Naturalist Private 4x4 Expedition* (Dedicated private vehicle, bespoke pacing, optical gear).
- **Card CTA:** "View Expedition Details".

## Section 5: The Sacred Coexistence (Rabari Feature)
- **Objective:** Cultural depth and ethical grounding.
- **Layout:** Full-width atmospheric visual banner of a Rabari shepherd against a crimson sunset, flanked by an editorial narrative card explaining the spiritual reverence for the goddess Chamunda and the leopard.

## Section 6: The Captains (Field Leadership)
- **Objective:** Humanize the brand and establish trust.
- **Content:** Introductions to the head naturalists and local trackers, highlighting their generational knowledge of leopard home ranges and commitment to ethical distances.

## Section 7: Curated Multi-Day Journeys
- **Objective:** Inspire multi-day stays and higher-value inquiries.
- **Layout:** Horizontal scroll timeline showcasing the "3-Day Essential Jawai Expedition" and "4-Day Wildlife & Photography Deep Dive".

## Section 8: Visual Journal & Sighting Dispatch
- **Objective:** Fresh editorial content and SEO authority.
- **Content:** Grid of 3 latest journal articles (e.g., *Understanding Leopard Behavior on Granite*, *Birding Jawai Bandh in Winter*, *A Guide to the Rabari Pastoralists*).

## Section 9: The Private Consultation / Journey Inquiry Section
- **Objective:** Primary conversion engine.
- **Layout:** Elegant embedded multi-step inquiry card with immediate field validation.

## Section 10: Footer & Ethical Tourism Disclosures
- **Content:** Detailed sitemap, official contact credentials, social links, conservation guidelines, copyright statement, and legal links.
