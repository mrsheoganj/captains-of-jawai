# Navigation Systems & Header Architecture

## 1. Desktop Navigation Bar
- **Sticky State:** Fixed top position, transition to translucent dark granite with blur on scroll.
- **Left:** Primary brand mark (`logo.PNG` transparent, 56px height) linking to `/`.
- **Center Menu Items (Editorial Hover Reveal):**
  1. *Expeditions* (Dropdown: Leopard Safari, Private 4x4, Birding & Wetland, Photography).
  2. *The Wilderness* (Dropdown: Granite Kopjes, Jawai Dam, Wildlife Guide, Seasons).
  3. *Coexistence* (Rabari Culture, The Story).
  4. *Journeys* (Curated Itineraries: 3-Day Classic, 4-Day In-Depth).
  5. *Field Journal* (Stories, Guides, Sighting Updates).
- **Right Utilities:**
  - Phone / Quick WhatsApp Trigger Icon.
  - Primary Conversion Button: **"Plan Your Journey"** (High-contrast Burnished Amber).

## 2. Mobile Navigation System (< 1024px)
- **Top Bar:** 64px height. Contains compact logo (left), direct WhatsApp action (center-right), and 2-line minimalist hamburger trigger (right).
- **Fullscreen Drawer:** Slides in with 300ms cubic-bezier transition.
  - Features 28px Cormorant Garamond typographic links.
  - Clear direct contact button: `+91 [CLIENT PHONE REQUIRED]` and WhatsApp link.
  - Direct inquiry CTA: "Start Your Journey Inquiry".
