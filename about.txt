Our Story Masters of the Granite Sanctuary
We do not believe in mass-market safari convoys, frantic chases or noisy tourism.
At Captains of Jawai, an expedition is a masterclass in reading nature: interpreting the sudden alarm call of a peacock echoing off the boulders, waiting patiently as the afternoon light turns granite into molten bronze, and watching a wild leopard emerge with regal stillness from her den.
What we believe
Mastery in the field. True tracking is an art of patience, acoustics, wind and animal behaviour.
Reverent coexistence. We honour the bond between the leopards and the Rabari community.
Uncompromising exclusivity. Small private groups, dedicated vehicles, no convoys.
How booking works
Send a safari request with your date, drive time and number of guests.
We reply personally, usually within 12 hours, with availability and the price.
You confirm directly with us. No online payments, no pressure.
We meet you at your stay or an agreed point in Jawai for the drive.
We run safaris only. We do not book hotels or arrange transfers.
Enquiries
