The Code of the Captains Conservation & Ethics
How we watch wildlife, and why it matters.
No guarantees, no chases. We never promise sightings and never pursue an animal.
Engines off. Vehicles are switched off during observation.
Distance and escape routes. We keep a respectful distance and never block an animal's path.
No baiting, calls or lights to attract wildlife.
Limited vehicles at a sighting; we wait our turn or move on.
Community first. We work with and pay local people fairly, and respect private land and shrines.
No drones around wildlife.
