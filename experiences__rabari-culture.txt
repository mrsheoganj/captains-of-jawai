The Living Pact Rabari Pastoral Heritage
The red-turbaned herders whose tolerance of the leopard shaped this land.
The Rabari are semi-nomadic pastoralists who have grazed their herds across these hills for generations. Their faith, centred on hill shrines and the goddess, extends a remarkable tolerance to the leopards that share their land.
What you will see on safari
On morning and evening drives you pass Rabari herds moving between the hills and the water, red turbans in the fields, and small shrines on the rocks where leopards often rest. Your tracker will explain how herders and leopards share the same ground.
Be a good guest
Keep a respectful distance from homes, herds and shrines.
Always ask before photographing people.
Stay on the tracks agreed with the village.
