FAQ Frequently asked questions
Wildlife, seasons, getting here and how booking works.
Safaris & Wildlife
Are leopard sightings guaranteed in Jawai?
No ethical operator can guarantee a wild animal sighting, and anyone who promises one is misleading you. However, because Jawai's leopards live on open granite rather than in dense forest, the probability of observing them over a 2–3 day stay is very high.
How is Jawai different from Ranthambhore or Jim Corbett?
Jawai is an open, lived-in landscape rather than a gated national park. There are no rigid zones or shared canters; drives are in private 4x4 vehicles with local trackers, and you can combine leopards with wetland birding and Rabari culture.
Is it safe to be near leopards in an open vehicle?
Yes. Jawai's leopards are habituated to people and vehicles. Our Captains keep respectful distances, never block an animal's path and switch off engines during observation.
What else will I see besides leopards?
Striped hyena, nilgai, chinkara, jungle cat, Indian grey wolf and desert fox are possible, along with mugger crocodiles and over 300 bird species, including winter flamingos and cranes.
Planning
When is the best time to visit?
October to March is prime season with cool mornings, basking leopards and migratory birds. April to June is hot but rewarding; the monsoon (July–September) is lush and dramatic.
How many drives should we book?
Three or four drives (morning and evening on two days) give a fair chance of good leopard sightings. Photographers often book more.
How do we reach Jawai?
Udaipur Airport is about 130 km (2.5 to 3 hours by road) and Jodhpur Airport about 175 km (3.5 hours). The nearest railway station, Sumerpur Jawai Bandh, is about 18 km away, and Falna about 37 km. We run safaris only and do not arrange transfers, but we are happy to suggest options.
Booking
Do you book hotels or transfers?
No. Captains of Jawai runs safaris only. You book your own stay; tell us where you are staying and we will arrange where to meet for each drive.
Can I book and pay online?
No. Send a safari request with your date and drive time and we will confirm availability and the price personally, usually within 12 hours. Nothing is charged until you confirm.
Is Jawai suitable for children and older travellers?
Very much so. Private vehicles let us set a gentle pace, shorten drives and plan comfortable breaks.
What should I wear?
Neutral colours, warm layers for winter mornings, a hat and closed shoes. Modest clothing is appreciated on village visits.
Have another question?
Write to us and we will answer personally.
