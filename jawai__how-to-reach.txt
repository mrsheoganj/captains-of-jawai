Jawai guide How to reach Jawai
Udaipur Airport is about 130 km away and Sumerpur Jawai Bandh railway station about 18 km. Routes by air, rail and road.
Jawai has no airport of its own, but it is well placed between Udaipur and Jodhpur and has a railway station on a main line.
By air
Udaipur (Maharana Pratap Airport, UDR) is the usual choice, with flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and other cities. It is about 130 km from Bera, around two and a half to three hours by road. Jodhpur Airport (JDH) is about 175 km, around three and a half hours. Ahmedabad has more international connections and is about five to six hours by road.
By train
Sumerpur Jawai Bandh (station code JWB) sits right by the dam, about 18 km from Bera. Falna (FA) is about 37 km away. Both are on the line between Ahmedabad and Jaipur, with trains to and from Delhi, Jaipur, Ajmer, Abu Road and Ahmedabad; Falna gets more express trains. The small Mori Bera halt, about 5 km from Bera, has only a few local trains.

Times depend on traffic and the route. The last part of the journey is on smaller country roads, so allow time and avoid arriving late at night if you can.
Getting to your safari
We run safaris only and do not arrange transfers or hotels. Most hotels in Jawai can send a car to the airport or station. Once you are here, tell us where you are staying when you book a safari and we will arrange where to meet.
Combining Jawai with other places
Jawai fits neatly into a Rajasthan route: Udaipur, then Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur, then Jawai, then on to Jodhpur. See places to visit near Jawai .
Questions people ask
What is the nearest airport to Jawai?
Udaipur (Maharana Pratap Airport), about 130 km or two and a half to three hours by road. Jodhpur Airport is about 175 km away.
What is the nearest railway station to Jawai?
Sumerpur Jawai Bandh (station code JWB), about 18 km from Bera. Falna, about 37 km away, has more express trains.
How far is Jawai from Udaipur?
About 115 km, around two and a half hours by car. Udaipur airport is about 130 km away.
How far is Jawai from Jodhpur?
Jodhpur airport is about 175 km away, around three and a half hours by car.
