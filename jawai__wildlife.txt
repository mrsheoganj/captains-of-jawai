Jawai guide Birds and wildlife of Jawai
Leopards get the attention, but Jawai also has hyenas, crocodiles, cranes, flamingos and a long list of raptors.
Jawai is dry, rocky country with one large body of water in the middle. That mix gives it a wider range of wildlife than its size suggests.
Mammals
Leopard : the top predator, living in the granite hills. See the leopards of Jawai .
Striped hyena : mostly nocturnal, occasionally seen at dusk or dawn.
Jungle cat and Indian fox : small hunters of the scrub and fields.
Nilgai (blue bull): the largest antelope in Asia, common in the farmland.
Grey (Hanuman) langur : troops on the rocks and temples. Their alarm calls often reveal a leopard.
Indian sloth bear : shaggy and mostly nocturnal, found in the rocky Aravalli country around Jawai and occasionally seen at dawn or dusk.
Indian wolf : small packs range across the open farmland and grassland; sightings are rare and always special.
Chinkara (Indian gazelle): a small, graceful gazelle of dry scrub and field edges.
Indian desert fox, Indian crested porcupine, ruddy and grey mongoose, Indian hare and wild boar.
Reptiles
The mugger crocodile is the one most visitors want to see. Jawai Bandh holds a large population, often estimated locally at several hundred. It basks on the reservoir banks and sandbars on cool, sunny days. Monitor lizards are also found on the rocks.
Birds
Bird lists for the Jawai area run to well over a hundred species. In winter the reservoir is the main attraction:
Bar-headed goose , which migrates over the Himalaya
Demoiselle crane and common crane
Greater flamingo
Sarus crane , the world's tallest flying bird, seen in pairs in the wetlands
Great white and Dalmatian pelicans in some winters
Ruddy shelduck, northern pintail and other ducks
Painted stork, Eurasian spoonbill, black-headed ibis and river tern
Around the hills and fields look for Indian peafowl, grey francolin, Indian eagle-owl on the rocks, Indian vulture and a good number of eagles, harriers and falcons, especially in winter.
When to see what
Migratory birds are present from about November to February. Mammals are easiest to see in the cool months, early and late in the day. The monsoon months bring breeding residents and green hills, but tracks can be hard going.
Questions people ask
What animals can you see in Jawai besides leopards?
Striped hyena, jungle cat, Indian fox, nilgai, grey langur, wild boar and mugger crocodiles, plus a large number of birds.
Which migratory birds come to Jawai?
Bar-headed geese, demoiselle and common cranes, greater flamingos, ruddy shelducks and many ducks and waders, mainly from November to February.
Are there sloth bears and wolves in Jawai?
Yes, both live in the wider Jawai area, but they are shy and mostly nocturnal, so sightings are uncommon. Striped hyenas, jungle cats, nilgai and chinkara are seen more often.
Are there tigers in Jawai?
No. There are no tigers or lions in Jawai. The leopard is the largest predator.
