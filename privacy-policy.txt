Legal Privacy Policy
What we collect
When you send an enquiry we collect the details you provide (name, email, phone, travel preferences and message) along with basic technical data such as IP address to prevent spam.
How we use it
Only to respond to your enquiry and plan your journey. We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.
Retention
Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations. Ask us at any time to access or delete your data.
Contact
Write to us using the details on our contact page.
