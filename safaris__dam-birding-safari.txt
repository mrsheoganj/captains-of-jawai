Avian Wetland Jawai Bandh Wetland & Raptor Drive
Flamingos, cranes and basking mugger crocodiles on the great reservoir.
Duration About 3 hours
Timings Morning 6:00–9:00 AM · Evening 4:00–7:00 PM
Best season November – February for migrants
Group Private or shared 4x4 gypsy, up to 6 guests
Check availability
Ask on WhatsApp Reply usually within 12 hours · nothing charged online
A slower, scope-led exploration of the reservoir shoreline, winter flamingo flocks, demoiselle cranes, raptors and the resident marsh crocodiles.
The largest reservoir in western Rajasthan
Commissioned by Maharaja Umaid Singh of Jodhpur in 1946 and completed in 1957, Jawai Bandh is a lifeline for people and wildlife alike. Each winter it becomes a staging ground on the Central Asian Flyway.
Key species
Greater and lesser flamingo
Demoiselle crane and bar-headed goose
Painted stork, Eurasian spoonbill, river tern
Osprey, steppe and tawny eagle, peregrine falcon
Mugger (marsh) crocodile
The experience
We work the water's edge slowly with binoculars and a spotting scope, stopping where the light and birds are best. It pairs beautifully with an evening leopard drive on the same day.
Highlights
Greater flamingo, demoiselle crane and bar-headed goose (winter)
Mugger crocodiles basking on sandy shoals
Osprey, eagles and falcons over the water
Spotting scope and field guides on board
