Photography Wildlife Photography Expedition
Rim-lit granite, golden hour and patient positioning.
Duration Multi-drive programmes
Timings Golden hours, morning and evening
Best season October – March (best light November – February)
Group Max 3 photographers per vehicle
Check availability
Ask on WhatsApp Reply usually within 12 hours · nothing charged online
Planned around light: low-angle positioning, beanbag mounts and a Captain who understands composition as well as tracking.
Light, patience and position
The granite domes of Jawai create extraordinary photographic stages: a leopard silhouetted against a dawn sky, warm rim light on spotted fur, a cub peering from a cave mouth. Your Captain plans each drive around the sun as much as the animals.
Recommended kit
Primary: 100–400mm, 150–600mm or a 500mm prime
Secondary: 24–70mm for environmental and landscape frames
Fast cards and spare batteries (charging available on board)
Drone flying is not permitted around wildlife.
Highlights
Vehicle limited to 3 photographers for clear angles
Beanbags and charging on board
Positioning for backlight and rim light on granite
Ethics first: no baiting, no calls, no crowding
