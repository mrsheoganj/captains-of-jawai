Bespoke Master Naturalist Private 4x4 Expedition
Your vehicle, your Captain, your pace.

A fully private expedition designed around you: flexible timings, longer sightings, boulder-top sundowners and a senior naturalist dedicated to your party.
Exclusivity changes everything
On a shared drive you follow the group. On a private expedition, the day bends around you: linger with a basking leopard for an hour, chase the best light for photographs, or pause for a picnic breakfast on the rocks.
Who it is for
Families with young children who need a gentler pace, couples celebrating something special, and serious photographers who want angles and patience a standard drive cannot offer.
Add-ons
Private sundowner on a secluded granite summit
Jawai Bandh wetland drive on the same day
We run safaris only. Hotels and transfers are not part of the service: tell us where you are staying and we will meet you there or at an agreed point.
