Jawai leopard safari: the complete guide

Everything about a Jawai leopard safari in one place: safari options, timings, price, how to book, what you will see, and how to watch leopards responsibly.

Jawai, in Pali district, Rajasthan, is one of the best places in India to see wild leopards. They live in the caves of the granite hills around the Jawai Dam, close to villages, farms and temples. This guide covers everything you need to plan your safari.

Safari options
SAFARI	BEST FOR	DURATION
Classic Leopard Tracking Safari	First-time visitors; the best chance of a leopard	About 3 hours, morning or evening
Jawai Bandh Wetland & Raptor Drive	Birders: flamingos, cranes, raptors and crocodiles	About 3 hours
Master Naturalist Private 4x4 Expedition	Flexible timings, longer sightings, special occasions	Half or full day
Wildlife Photography Expedition	Photographers who want the best light and positioning	Multi-drive

Every drive is in an open 4x4 gypsy, private to your group (up to six guests), with a driver and an experienced local tracker.

Timings
Morning safari: 6:00 to 9:00 AM. Cool air, soft light, leopards returning to the rocks after the night.
Evening safari: 4:00 to 7:00 PM. Leopards come out onto the warm granite; golden light for photos.

Start times shift a little with sunrise and sunset through the year. Not sure which to choose? Read morning vs evening safari in Jawai.

Leopard safaris start from ₹3,999 per jeep for your group, so the cost per person falls as more people share the jeep. Longer private and photography safaris are priced on request. Details: Jawai safari price.

What you will see
Leopards on granite outcrops, in caves and near hill temples; read about the leopards of Jawai.
Mugger crocodiles basking on the banks of the Jawai Dam.
Birds: flamingos, demoiselle cranes and bar-headed geese in winter, plus raptors, peafowl and many residents.
Other wildlife: striped hyenas, jackals, nilgai and more, depending on luck. See birds and wildlife of Jawai.
Rabari herders with their flocks, living alongside the leopards as they have for generations.
Will you see a leopard?

Jawai offers excellent leopard sightings, but no honest operator can guarantee one: leopards are wild animals. Your chances rise with every drive, so we recommend two or three safaris over a stay of one or two nights. What happens on the drive: your first Jawai leopard safari.

Responsible tourism

Jawai's leopards are not inside a fenced national park; they share the land with people. That makes good behaviour essential:

Engines off at sightings and a respectful distance from every animal.
No baiting, calling, flash photography or drones.
Never block a leopard's path or crowd a sighting.
Respect villagers, herders and temples; ask before photographing people.
