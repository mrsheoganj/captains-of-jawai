JAWAI TRAVEL GUIDE
Things to do in Jawai, Rajasthan

Leopard safaris at dawn and dusk, birds and crocodiles at the dam, hill temples, Rabari villages, sunsets on the granite and day trips to Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh.

Most visitors build their days around a dawn and a dusk safari. The hours in between, and the days around them, are full of things to see. Here are the best experiences in and around Jawai.

Wildlife experiences
1. Go on a leopard safari

The reason most people come. An open 4x4 drive with a local tracker through the granite hills, at 6:00 AM or 4:00 PM. See the complete Jawai leopard safari guide.

2. Watch birds at the Jawai Dam

From about November to February the reservoir hosts flamingos, demoiselle cranes, bar-headed geese and many ducks and waders. The wetland drive is made for birders.

3. Spot mugger crocodiles

Crocodiles haul out on the sandbanks and rocks of the Jawai Dam, especially on sunny winter afternoons.

4. Photograph leopards on the granite

Leopards silhouetted on ridges at sunset are a Jawai speciality. Read our photography tips.

Culture and temples
5. Climb to a hill temple

The Dev Giri cave temple, where leopards are often seen on the rocks around the steps, and the shrines overlooking the dam are the best known.

6. Meet the Rabari herders

Learn how the Rabari have shared these hills with leopards for generations, on a respectful, arranged visit. See Rabari heritage and Jawai culture.

7. Walk the village lanes of Bera

Fields, small shrines and granite hills right behind the houses: the best way to understand how close people and leopards live here.

8. Try local food

Dal baati, ker sangri and other Marwari dishes are served at many homestays and hotels around Jawai.

Slow moments
9. Watch the sunset from a granite outcrop

Climbing a rock for the last light of the day is a Jawai tradition. See boulder sundowners.

10. Sunrise at the dam

Before or after a morning drive, the dam is calm and misty in winter, with birds on the water and the granite domes reflected beyond.

Day trips
11. Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh

The 15th-century marble Jain temple at Ranakpur (about 50 km) and the huge walls of Kumbhalgarh Fort (about 80 km), part of the UNESCO-listed hill forts of Rajasthan, make an easy day out between safaris.

12. Mount Abu

Rajasthan's only hill station, with the Dilwara temples and Nakki Lake, is about 100 km away.
